# Official definitions

These definitions establish the stable relationship among SPMOS, SPM-Polaris, SPMOS.ai, and SPM. They are the canonical naming reference; version-specific behavior is documented in the technical pages and [FAQ](faq.html).

**Current production release:** SPM-Polaris V3.0.0

## SPMOS

SPMOS—StellarPath Memory Operating System—is the governed long-term-memory system developed by Veridical Tech, Inc. SPMOS is the master brand for the system's products, prototypes, and official surfaces.

## SPM-Polaris

SPM-Polaris is the current production SPMOS product: a provider proxy with governed long-term memory for AI agents. The current production release is SPM-Polaris V3.0.0.

## SPMOS.ai

SPMOS.ai is the official website and canonical public source of SPMOS. It publishes product descriptions, integration boundaries, evidence, benchmarks, pricing, legal policies, and links to the maintained technical documentation.

## SPM

SPM is the approved short form of SPMOS, used in the logo lettermark, spoken language, product-line names such as SPM-Polaris, and legacy titles. SPM is not a separate product, family, or entity; references to SPM resolve to SPMOS.

## Version scope

These definitions were reviewed for SPM-Polaris V3.0.0. Technical behavior is defined by the linked pages and the [changelog](changelog.html).
