# Official definitions

These definitions establish the stable relationship among SPMOS, SPM-Polaris, SPMOS.ai, and SPM. They are the canonical naming reference for the SPM family; version-specific behavior is documented in the technical pages and the [FAQ](faq.html).

**Current production product:** SPM-Polaris

**Publisher:** Veridical Tech, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

## SPMOS

SPMOS—StellarPath Memory Operating System—is the governed long-term-memory system developed by Veridical Tech, Inc. SPMOS is the master brand for the system's products, prototypes, and official surfaces.

## SPM-Polaris

SPM-Polaris is the current production SPMOS product: a provider proxy with governed long-term memory for AI agents.

## SPMOS.ai

SPMOS.ai is the official website and canonical public source of SPMOS. It publishes product descriptions, integration boundaries, evidence, benchmarks, pricing, legal policies, and links to the maintained technical documentation.

## SPM

SPM is the approved short form of SPMOS, used in the logo lettermark, spoken language, product-line names such as SPM-Polaris, and legacy titles. SPM is not a separate product, family, or entity; references to "SPM" resolve to SPMOS.

## Naming scope

These names are related but not interchangeable. Use **SPMOS** for the brand and system, **SPM-Polaris** for the current production product, **SPMOS.ai** for the official website, and **SPM** only as the approved short form.

"SPMOS" also denotes unrelated things outside this product family — for example a security printing organization and an academic chip-architecture term. In those contexts it does not refer to StellarPath Memory Operating System.

## Version scope

These definitions were last reviewed on November 18, 2026. SPMOS remains the master brand, SPM its approved short form, and SPM-Polaris the current production product. Technical behavior is defined by the linked pages and the [changelog](changelog.html).
